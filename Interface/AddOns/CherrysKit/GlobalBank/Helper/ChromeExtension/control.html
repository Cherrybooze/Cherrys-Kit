      Protect your Gameforge accounts

      Choose a password. Accounts and their links are encrypted with it and a random key this page makes, and you'll enter it to open them. If you forget it, nobody can recover them. Your items, gold and characters aren't encrypted and don't need it.

      Moving from another PC? Load your backup file here, then enter your encryption key:

      Enter your encryption key

      There's an encrypted accounts file, but this Chrome doesn't have its key. Paste the key you copied on your old PC. Your password opens them after that.

      A different file?

      Enter your password at the top of this page to open your Gameforge accounts.

      Gameforge accounts

      These are encrypted on this PC, and open only with your password and this Chrome's encryption key. They stay open until you lock them or close this page. Other extensions, web pages and game addons can't read them, and the helper only ever gets the encrypted file.

      Link characters

      Nothing links by itself. Filter by part of a name, tick what you want (Shift-click ticks a whole range), pick an account and press Link. You can also paste names.

      Moving to another PC

      Copy your encryption key first. The file travels with the helper's folder (GameforgeAccounts.json), but the key stays in this Chrome. On the new PC you'll need the file, the key and your password. Without the key, nothing can open the file.
