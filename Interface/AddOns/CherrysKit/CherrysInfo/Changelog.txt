Cherry's Info

1.0.4 (2026-11-20)
- "Show all client buffs and debuffs" (Target & World tab, off by default): hides the game's player
  buff frame and draws every buff and debuff the client holds for the player (UnitBuff / UnitDebuff,
  no count limit on the client side) from 96 icons in AllBuffs.xml. Buffs, then debuffs on a new row,
  then (with "Include hidden effects") the entries of UnitBuffInfo's whole list that no shown effect
  accounts for. Timer and stack count, game tooltip, right-click cancels a buff, Shift+drag moves.
  Read after UNIT_BUFF_CHANGED for the player (0.1 s gathering) and every 3 s as a fallback; timers
  twice a second from cached texts.
- /cinfo buffs: counts the effects the client holds against the game's own buff icons.

1.0.1 (2026-11-01)
- Overhead health bars build their frame names once, not on every pass.
- CherrysQuests.xml: three tab labels had a bare &, which XML doesn't allow.

1.0 (2026-10-31)
- Cherry's Quests, pbInfo, TooltipIds, PortraitHP and Extended Macro Icons in one addon and one folder,
  with one switch in /ck and one window (/cinfo, /cq) with four tabs. Saved settings carry over.

Cherry's Quests

1.0.0 (2026-10-30)
- First version for Cherry's Kit: a quest tracker with font, outline, size, scale, width,
  background, sort and progress-bar options (/cq). Ctrl+click a quest cycles through the NPCs
  its texts name and puts the one picked in the game's NPC tracking list; the pick is kept per
  character and quest. Replaces the game's and pbInfo's trackers while shown.
