Cherry's Bar

1.1.2
  The top bar now leaves the game's frames alone: it is drawn under the portrait, housing controls,
  target, buffs and minimap, and its displays stay in the clear stretch between them. Clear left and
  Clear right are set per bar (400 and 490 on top). Move frames is now per bar, off for the top and on
  for the bottom. When on, it takes the portrait's separate buttons and the housing frame along.

1.1.1
  Art now comes straight from Cherry's finished Studio Panel Assets pack: Ironstone, Blacksteel,
  Froststeel, Darkwood, Verdant and Arcane, with its icons, controls and 2:1 volume speakers. Saved
  style names carry over. Bars default to 24 high, with text and icons centred clear of the rail.
  The options window's - and + are the pack's pictures.

1.1.0
  All of XBar's pictures are gone: six bar styles and a full icon set from Cherry's sheets, drawn as
  seamless tiles, plus plain Slate. Moving displays is now clear: a map of both bars in /cbar where displays
  can be dragged or moved with buttons, dragging on the bars themselves while unlocked, and a tick
  list on right-clicking a bar. New displays: Currencies, Durability, Daily quests and Volume, split
  out of Money, Damage and Class or following Titan Panel's ideas. New figures: gold and XP per hour,
  time to level, free bag slots, addon memory, map position. Auto-hide per bar, and a bar height
  setting. Settings from 1.0.0 start again from the defaults.

1.0.0
  First version, forked from XBar III 1.72 for Cherry's Kit. Top bar, bottom bar or both, top only by
  default. Displays set per bar and side, with right-click options. Reads only on events or slow
  timers, and redraws only when a text changes.
