What Cherry's Bar uses from XBar III
  - The bar skins in Media/Skins (Black, Gloss, Blockz, AdPanel, Tribal) and the icons in Media/Icons.
  - The readings, tokens, game-window menu lists and frame positions of the displays, rewritten.
  - Not included: XBar's add-on manager, translations, and its bonus modules (damage log, threat
    meter, minimap, raid save, web links, casting bar, item preview).

Changes made by Cherry (2026): two bars instead of one; displays flow along each bar and are set per
bar; right-click settings; a new options window; new default skin (Media/Skins/Slate, drawn by
tools/gen_cherrysbar_art.py); reading only on events or slow timers. See Changelog.txt.

Titan Panel
  The layout of the bars and the settings (top and bottom bars, displays set per bar, right-click
  options for icon, label and colour) follow the ideas of Titan Panel for World of Warcraft, by the
  Titan Panel Development Team. Titan Panel's licence allows redistribution only without
