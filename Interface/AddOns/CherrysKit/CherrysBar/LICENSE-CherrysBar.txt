What Cherry's Bar uses from XBar III
  - The readings, tokens, game-window menu lists and frame positions of the displays, rewritten.
  - None of XBar's pictures.
  - Not included: XBar's add-on manager, translations, and its bonus modules (damage log, threat
    meter, minimap, raid save, web links, casting bar, item preview, low-detail switch).

Pictures
  The bar styles (except Slate) and every icon in Media were made by Cherry for this addon, from the
  sheets in the repository's design/cherrys-bar folder. Slate is a gradient drawn by
  tools/gen_cherrysbar_art.py. They are offered under the licence above.

Changes made by Cherry (2026): two bars instead of one; displays flow along each bar and are set per
bar; a map of the bars, dragging and right-click settings; auto-hide; new displays and figures; a new
options window; new pictures; reading only on events or slow timers. See Changelog.txt.

Titan Panel
  The layout of the bars and the settings (top and bottom bars, displays set per bar, right-click
  options for icon, label and colour, auto-hide, per-hour figures) follow the ideas of Titan Panel for
  World of Warcraft, by the Titan Panel Development Team. Titan Panel's licence allows redistribution
  only without modification, so Cherry's Bar contains none of its code, pictures or other files.
  Titan Panel has not endorsed this addon.
