Cherry's Quests

1.0.0 (2026-10-30)
- First version for Cherry's Kit: a quest tracker with font, outline, size, scale, width,
  background, sort and progress-bar options (/cq). Ctrl+click a quest cycles through the NPCs
  its texts name and puts the one picked in the game's NPC tracking list; the pick is kept per
  character and quest. Replaces the game's and pbInfo's trackers while shown.
