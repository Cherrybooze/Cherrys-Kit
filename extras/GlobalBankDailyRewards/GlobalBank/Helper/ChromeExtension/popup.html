How collection works

Open the daily loyalty page through ZShop in game to collect. With this on, the extension clicks an available reward on Gameforge's daily page. With it off, click the reward yourself and it is still recorded.

A collection is saved only once the page confirms it, and needs a matching character snapshot at level 100 or above. Collection can succeed on Gameforge even when saving it here fails.

New reward day comes from the calendar and the last saved confirmation, not from Gameforge. It doesn't prove a reward is waiting.

Automated use may be against Gameforge's terms of service; use it at your own risk.
