        Currencies

        How this works

        Every currency any of your characters has gets a column. Click a heading to sort by it, and a character to select it. A dash means not recorded: that character needs one login on the current version, and a 0 is a real zero. Diamonds and Rubies belong to an account, so the table doesn't add them up, and the cards above count each RoM account once. Daily Tokens are each character's own.

        Stats

        How this works

        Click a heading to sort by it. Stat names and effects use the colours saved from the game's tooltips. Mana stones include fusion stones. The counts follow the region and the server ticks at the top. Click a character to select it. Right-click a stat, or focus it and press Shift+F10, to exclude it. Exclusions apply to this Stats page and are saved only in this Chrome profile.

          No gold A full cap of 999,999,999
          How this works

          Red is no gold, orange is half a cap, and bright green is a full cap, marked MAX. Click the three squares beside the colour bar to pick your own colours, and use Name letters for smaller tiles. Hover a character for its details, click to select it. Right-click to copy its name, Gameforge account or password. Each box is a Gameforge account you linked on the Gameforge accounts page, with a region for each of its servers, named on top, and its RoM accounts in them, so a full account shows as a block of green and the colours in between are what's left to collect. Servers leaves one off this map, or the names off the regions. A mailbox after a name is gold waiting in its mailbox, with a red ! when some of its mail goes within 48 hours: see the Mail page. A balance says nothing about whether the saved data is current: see the Characters page for that.

          Mail

          How this works

          The game only shows a mailbox while it's open, so Global Bank keeps what each one held then, and every mail your characters send, the newest 360. Mail sent to one of your characters after its mailbox was last open is added to it as an estimate, until that mailbox is opened again. A mail waits 30 days before the game sends it back: the time left for one seen in a mailbox is the game's own, and for one sent since it's counted from when it was sent. A red ! marks mail that goes within 48 hours. Mail with nothing on it isn't counted. The counts follow the region and the server ticks at the top. Click a name to select that character.

          Gameforge accounts

          Protect your Gameforge accounts

          Choose a password. Accounts and their links are encrypted with it and a random key this page makes, and you'll enter it to open them. If you forget it, nobody can recover them. Your items, gold and characters aren't encrypted and don't need it.

          Moving from another PC? Load your backup file here, then enter your encryption key:

          Enter your encryption key

          There's an encrypted accounts file, but this Chrome doesn't have its key. Paste the key you copied on your old PC. Your password opens them after that.

          A different file?

          Your Gameforge accounts are locked. Your items, gold and characters don't need them.

          Accounts

          These are encrypted on this PC, and open only with your password and this Chrome's encryption key. They stay open until you lock them or close this page. Other extensions, web pages and game addons can't read them, and the helper only ever gets the encrypted file.

          Link characters

          How this works

          Nothing links by itself. Each box is one Gameforge account on one server, and the characters not linked yet come after them by server. Each character shows its name and its RoM account, the characters one login screen lists; a dash means no login screen has listed it yet, and logging in any character through Cherry's Loginxml lists its account. Click characters to select them (Shift-click selects a range, Select takes a whole box), pick an account and press Link selected. Right-click a character to select its whole RoM account. You can also paste names.

          Moving to another PC

          Copy your encryption key first. The file travels with the helper's folder (GameforgeAccounts.json), but the key stays in this Chrome. On the new PC you'll need the file, the key and your password. Without the key, nothing can open the file.

          Characters

          How this works

          Click a heading to sort by it, a character to select it, and a group's Select to select all of it. Selecting doesn't change the Bank until you press Use in Bank. A character needing a login was saved by an older version: log in with it once. A bank not recorded also takes one login with that character. Update now only reads the saved files, so it can't do either. The search also takes filters, with commas or spaces between them: #level>=100, #classes>3 (how many classes), #classlevels>500 (their levels added up), #gold<1m, #dailytokens>5k, #charges>10k (Arcane Transmutor), #diamonds, #rubies, #stones, #bagfree>10, #bankfree, #worn, #mail, #mailgold>1m, #class=mage, and any currency by the start of its name, such as #honor>1000. k, m and b are thousands, millions and billions; a filter with no number, such as #charges, means more than none; #needslogin, #nobank, #steam and #linked are yes or no. Transfer stones counts Server Transfer Stones in the bag and bank, and shows "at least" while the bank isn't recorded. Reward day and Reward taken show the last daily reward collected through this extension, in your local time, for characters of level 100 or higher.

          Excluded characters

          The helper doesn't track these, so they don't appear anywhere on this page. Their game save files aren't changed. Right-click a character anywhere on this page and choose Stop tracking to add one here.

            Servers

            Servers and regions

            How this works

            The servers each region lists. The Region button at the top shows only its region's servers, and a server no region lists shows in every region. Move a server with its menu, add one the game's lists don't have, or take one you never play off its list. This only changes what this page shows.

            Characters by server

            How this works

            Each box is a RoM account: the characters one login screen lists. Click a character to select it, and right-click it to rename its RoM account or its Gameforge account. A character saved by an older version may not know its server: set it here, and it keeps that until it logs in once on the current version. Dimmed names are on a login screen's list but haven't saved anything here yet. This follows the region and the server ticks at the top.

            Macros

            How this works

            Every macro your characters have, read from their save folders, so no character needs to log in. Group them, search them, and write new ones. In game, the Import button in the macro window (/emi) brings in any of them with no need to close anything. Changing a character's own macros from here needs the game closed, and keeps a copy of the file it replaces.

            Library

            Macro window

            Click a macro, or drag one from the library onto a slot.

Compare them with the backups and put back one addon's settings or the whole file. Close the game first, because it saves over these files when a character logs out.

            Addons
            
      
            
        
            How this works
            Check for updates reads the latest Cherry’s Kit release on GitHub. Update and Download and install fetch that release’s files in a window of their own, check each one against the release’s checksum, and move the old version to Interface\offloaded_addons first, the same way the guided setup does. Offload moves an addon to Interface\offloaded_addons, where the game doesn’t load it, and Restore puts it back. Delete sends it to the Recycle Bin. Saved settings are kept either way. Each of these changes, installs and the mailbox switch included, asks Windows for administrator permission first, so a protected game folder can’t stop it part way; if you say No, nothing changes. An addon the kit has taken over, such as pbInfo or TooltipIds (now Cherry’s Info) or Cherry’s Loginxml (now part of LogNextChar), says what replaced it and offers Switch: the new addon installs if it isn’t here, then the old folder is offloaded. Close the game before installing.
