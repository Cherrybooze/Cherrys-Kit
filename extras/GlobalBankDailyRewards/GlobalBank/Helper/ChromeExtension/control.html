Read from folders instead of the helper

On a PC without the helper, the Bank tab can read your characters and icons straight from these two folders. It only reads them, and nothing leaves this PC. The Terminal and Auctions tabs still need the helper.

Usually Documents\Runes of Magic.
The folder Runes of Magic is installed in, for item icons. Optional.

Region settings

Europe and North America are separate games: characters and items can't move between them. Pick yours and every tab shows only its servers. Characters whose server isn't known yet always show.

No characters selected

Click any character on any tab to select it, Shift-click to select a range. Hover one for its details, right-click to copy its login or rename its accounts.

Global Bank is also open in another tab. Close one of them: changes to your Gameforge accounts in two tabs overwrite each other.

        Favourites

        Right-click any item on the Bank tab and choose Add to favourites. Click one here to find it on the Bank tab, and a name to select that character. The counts follow the region and the server ticks at the top.

        Currencies

        Every currency any of your characters has gets a column. Click a heading to sort by it, and a character to select it. A dash means not recorded yet: that character needs one login on the current version. Diamonds, Bonus and Billdin are per account, so they aren't added up.

        Stats

        Click a heading to sort by it. Stats appear once a character has logged in and out on the current version. Mana stones include fusion stones. The counts follow the region and the server ticks at the top. Click a name to select that character.

        Gold heatmap

        No gold500,000,000 is orange, a full cap of 999,999,999 is bright green

        Hover a character for its details, click to select it. Right-click to copy its name, Gameforge account or password. Characters are grouped by the accounts you link on the Gameforge accounts tab.

        Protect your Gameforge accounts

        Choose a password. Accounts and their links are encrypted with it and a random key this page makes, and you'll enter it to open them. If you forget it, nobody can recover them. Your items, gold and characters aren't encrypted and don't need it.

        Gameforge accounts

        These are encrypted on this PC, and open only with your password and this Chrome's encryption key. They stay open until you lock them or close this page. Other extensions, web pages and game addons can't read them, and the helper only ever gets the encrypted file.

        Link characters

        Nothing links by itself. Each box is one Gameforge account on one server, and the characters not linked yet come after them by server. Each character shows its name and its RoM account, the characters one login screen lists; a dash means no login screen has listed it yet, and logging in any character through Cherry's Loginxml lists its account. Click characters to select them (Shift-click selects a range, Select takes a whole box), pick an account and press Link selected. Right-click a character to select its whole RoM account. You can also paste names.

        Moving to another PC

        Copy your encryption key first. The file travels with the helper's folder (GameforgeAccounts.json), but the key stays in this Chrome. On the new PC you'll need the file, the key and your password. Without the key, nothing can open the file.

        Characters

        Click a heading to sort by it, a character to select it, and a group's Select to select all of it. A dot marks a character saved by an older version: log in with it once. Reward day and Reward taken show the last daily reward collected through this extension, in your local time, for characters of level 100 or higher.

        Servers and regions

        The servers each region lists. The Region button at the top shows only its region's servers, and a server no region lists shows in every region. Move a server with its menu, add one the game's lists don't have, or take one you never play off its list. This only changes what this page shows.

        Characters by server

        Each box is a RoM account: the characters one login screen lists. Click a character to select it, and right-click it to rename its RoM account or its Gameforge account. A character saved by an older version may not know its server: set it here, and it keeps that until it logs in once on the current version. Dimmed names are on a login screen's list but haven't saved anything here yet. This follows the region and the server ticks at the top.

        Macros

        Every macro your characters have, read from their save folders, so no character needs to log in. Group them, search them, and write new ones. In game, the Import button in the macro window (/emi) brings in any of them with no need to close anything. Changing a character's own macros from here needs the game closed, and keeps a copy of the file it replaces.

        Library

        Macro window

        Click a macro, or drag one from the library onto a slot.

        Addon settings

        The settings each addon saved, such as your auras in RoM WeakAuras and your rotations in KittyCombo. Most are shared by all characters; a few belong to one character. Compare them with the backups and put back one addon's settings or the whole file. Close the game first, because it saves over these files when a character logs out.
